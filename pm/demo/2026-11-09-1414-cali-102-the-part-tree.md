### 2026-11-09 · The part tree (CALI-102 #94, slice a)
1. Open a design: upper left, "Parts", each part with its pieces, a swatch by material, and the materials legend.
2. Click a piece's eye: it goes from the viewport, the row dims, the cards don't change. "hidden pieces are still
   checked" is always there. Reload: it's still hidden.
3. Click a piece: the chip reads "part · piece". Type a message and it's about that piece.
4. Double-click a part's name, type "Bar stool", Enter: the tree, chip and cards say Bar stool. The files keep the id.
5. "one body" in the view bar goes to the single mesh and back.
6. Offline: `PYTHONPATH=src python -m pytest -rs tests/test_part_tree.py`.
