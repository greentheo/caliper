### 2026-11-20 · CALI-183 (#294) · Copy… and Move… on a part's menu, placed by a typed form

**Right-click a part** (in the view or the tree; long-press on a phone) and choose **Copy…** or **Move…**. A small form
opens in the menu, in the design's units:
- **Along** x, y, z: from where it is;
- **Its middle at** x, y, z: filled with where its middle is now;
- **Beside** a part, on one of its sides (+x, −x, +y, −y, above, below): touching that part's box, the other two
  directions as they were;
- **Turn** 0, 90, 180 or 270° about z, x or y, about its own middle, so it turns in place.

**Copy it** makes `step_stool_2` (named "Step stool 2" if the original has a name; rename it as any part). It starts
beside the original on +x. It's a part of its own: no mirror, no FOLLOWS. The reply says how to link them if you want
("make step_stool_2 a mirror of step_stool"). It brings the original's loads as edited, its grain and its piece names.
It isn't checked yet, and the cards say so.

**Move it** writes where the part sits, without a new version of its script. The bar at the foot offers **Put back**.
The history keeps where it was. A mirror and its source can't be moved alone (it's greyed, with why), since the mirror
wouldn't follow.

**Both are measured where they land** against every other part: "it overlaps b by 6.0 cm³", "it touches step_stool", or
"it doesn't touch or overlap another part". Both are said in the chat log too.

**Tried on** a scratch copy of step-stool-5 on 8021: Copy → beside the original, touching. Move 20 in along y → Put back
returned it. A quarter turn about z with 600 mm along y showed its plywood pieces turned, where it sits.
