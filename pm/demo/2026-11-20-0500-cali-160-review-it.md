### 2026-11-20 · CALI-160 (#238) · Review it: Claude's reading, on your press

The button is caliper-ui's next. Until it's in, here's what it will do:
- **After a build, press Review it** ("one Claude call, about 30–60 s"). Claude reads every part against what you asked for.
- **Under Things to fix,** each thing it reads wrong is a row badged **Claude's reading**, with the change it proposes.
- **Tick one and Fix these.** It comes back **done, not measured**, never "fixed": nothing measures a reading.
- **Once the part has changed,** the row says "read on v1; it's changed since", and the button reads **Review again**.
- **An empty reading** says it found nothing more, and that it's a reading, not a check.
