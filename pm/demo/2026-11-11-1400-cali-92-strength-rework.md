### 2026-11-11 · CALI-92 (#70) · the strength and measurement items, reworked

**Carve a notch under a held edge.** On the stool with a plywood top and printed sides, cut the print away under the
middle of one side, just below the top. Before, that step read 1.66 where the part as drawn read 0.57. Now it reads
0.57, the same as drawn, and the carve's notes say: "A step that carves away what holds up an edge of the plywood keeps the
plywood's number as drawn: taking support away can't make it stronger."

**Measure the sample stool.** Its trial card says to measure "half an inch past the foot toward a short end, from a
straightedge resting on the two long edges", and gives the sag for that spot, 0.78 of the middle's. It adds that laid on
the short edges and read toward a long edge, it sags about 77% of that.
