### 2026-11-20 · CALI-102 (#94) · move a part in the tree without a mouse: Alt+arrows, and the menu's Move in the tree ▸

**With the keyboard.** Click a part's name in the tree (or Tab to it), then:
- **Alt+↑ / Alt+↓** moves it past the part above or below it, at its own level, with everything under it;
- **Alt+→** puts it under the part above it, as that part's last;
- **Alt+←** takes it out of the part it sits under, to just after that part.

The focus stays on the part, so you can press again. Where it can't go, nothing happens. Alt+← never sends the browser
Back from a part's row.

**From its menu.** Right-click a part (long-press on a phone) and choose **Move in the tree ▸**. It lists only the moves
that can happen, by name ("Put it under frame", "Take it out of frame"), each with its keys. On a phone, the drawer
now says "Hold a part to move it" in place of "Moving parts needs a wider window".

As with a drag, a move changes only how the tree shows the parts: no script, version or check.

had covered only in node tests.
