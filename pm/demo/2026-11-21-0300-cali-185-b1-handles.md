### 2026-11-21 · CALI-185 (#296, slice B1) · see what it must hold, on the model, and pick where

Select a part, open **Will it work? → Scenarios and supports**. While it's open:
- **On the model:** each load case is an amber box with an arrow the way it pushes (longer for more). A case that's off
  is grey. What holds the part is blue: a support's box, or the floor under it.
- **Typing a row's numbers** moves its box and arrow as you type.
- **Pick on the model** (on any load or support row), then click a face of the part: that row's box becomes the face.
  Esc stops. Save them to keep it: a new version, and its checks are out of date, as for any edit.

**Offline:** `python -m pytest tests/test_scenario_handles.py`.

**Slice B2: drag them.** Each load has two handles on the model: its box's middle and its arrow's white tail.
- **Drag the middle** to move the box over the part. It sits on the surface under the pointer, and "where: …" shows as
  you go.
- **Drag the tail** to turn the arrow. It snaps to down, front, back, left or right within 8°, and "which way: …"
  shows as you go.
- The rows follow. Save them to keep it.
