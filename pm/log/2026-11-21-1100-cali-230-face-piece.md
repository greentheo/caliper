### 2026-11-21 CALI-230 (the #376 follow-up): a picked face says which piece it's on, and which way it faces

— night shift (UI lane), 2026-11-21, on the foreman's word. It was held as a local branch until #376 merged.

**Why:** #376's Pick on the model wrote "Picked the flat face, 1.8 in up" on step-stool-5's tread: what kind of face and
how high, never whose. The chat's Face pick (#109) says the same.

**What's built (server):**
- **`joints.piece_namer(store, pid)`:** the piece a point in the script's frame is on. It uses the same test as
  `joints.namer`: a piece's surface within twice the tolerance. A seam where two pieces are about as near is no one
  piece. The piece is named as the tree names it (`tree.pieces`: the person's label, else its key).
  - The joints key a second "side" "side #2" and the tree keys it "side 2"; that's mapped.
- **`features`:**
  - A flat face keeps its outward normal, as placed (`normalAt`, turned by the pose), and a point surely on it (its
    biggest triangle's centroid: a ring's centre is in its hole).
  - `SCHEMA` is 4, so older files are worked out again.
- **`said`:** "face: the tread's top face, 400 mm up". A level face is its top face or its underside, and any other is as
  before. A part with no pieces: "face: the top face, …".
- **The features route** passes `piece=joints.piece_namer(...)`.
- With this in, #376's rows read "Picked the tread's top face, 1.8 in up", with no change to #376: it strips "face: " (pinned).

**Judgment calls:**
- **Top and underside only for level faces (within about 11°).** Front, back, left and right depend on which way the part
  faces, which nothing declares; those are said "flat face" as before.
- **The face's point is a triangle's centroid, not its centre**, so an L-shaped or ringed face is never named by a point
  in the air.

**Tests:** `tests/test_face_piece.py`, 9. test_features' route pin now counts its four flat faces, plus a top and an
underside. test_joint_picks passes.

**Mutants (8/8 caught):** no piece passed; no seam test; no key mapping; the normal not posed; the inside point the
centre; top and underside swapped; labels ignored; the route without the namer.

**Round 2 (reviewer-2's should-fix and smalls).**
- **A face fused across two pieces names both.** The union fuses coplanar faces, and the name came from one point. The
  stool's front (the tread's front and the side's front edge, an L of about equal areas) read "the tread's" wherever it was
  clicked. Each face now keeps points over its 16 biggest triangles (four each: the centroid, and halfway to each corner),
  each with the area it stands for, in the part's own frame. A piece holding 15% or more of that area is named, biggest
  first:
  - "face: the flat face across the tread and the side, …";
  - a seam's point counts for neither piece;
  - a sliver under 15% isn't named. The x = 0 end is the side's, though the tread's end is under 5% of it.
- **SCHEMA 5**, for the points.
- **Smalls:**
  - a posed fixture (moved, turned and lifted 50 mm): the tread's top is still its top face, at 450 mm;
  - a 30° slope is a flat face, never "the top face".

  and so is the sampling's shape.
