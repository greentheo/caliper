### 2026-11-20 · CALI-102 (#94) · move a part in the tree without a mouse: Alt+arrows, and Move in the tree ▸

#204 (slice b) left no keyboard way to move a part, and none at all at phone width, where rows can't be dragged.

- **`nudge(design, id, dir)`** turns one step into the drop the drag already makes (`[target, zone]` for `moveInTree`), so a
  move saves `part_tree` and nothing else, as #204's is pinned to:
  - **up / down:** past the part beside it at its own level, taking what sits under it along;
  - **in:** under the part above it, as that part's last;
  - **out:** out of the part it sits under, to just after it;
  - `null` where there's nowhere to go. A parent that's gone doesn't count.
- **The keys (`treeKey`, now the tree's one keydown listener):** Alt+↑ ↓ → ← on a part's row. The focus stays on the moved
  part, so the keys go on moving it. They do nothing on a piece's row, with Ctrl or ⌘, while renaming, or while a design loads.
  On a part's row the key is always taken, even where the part can't go, because Alt+← is the browser's Back. The rename
  box keeps its Enter and Escape. The rows say their keys (`aria-keyshortcuts`).
- **The part menu has Move in the tree ▸** (before Versions ▸) where any move can happen. Its submenu lists only those
  moves, by name ("Put it under frame", "Take it out of frame"), each with its keys. On a phone that's the long-press, and
  the drawer says "Hold a part to move it" in place of "Moving parts needs a wider window".
