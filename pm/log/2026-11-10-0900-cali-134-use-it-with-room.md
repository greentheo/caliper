### 2026-11-10 · CALI-134 (#167) · Use it offers a step the re-check can see

**What was wrong.** Use it offered the deepest step that passed the study's own check. The re-check on the rebuilt part often read far lower.

**What decides the gap is the walls, not the number.** A lighter load scales every step's number and leaves the shape alone, so a thin step can clear any margin: a bridge at 15 lb read 3.06 at its deepest step and re-checked at 0.94. So a study now records each passing step's thinnest wall, and whether a check at that shape's own voxels could see it. The offered step is the deepest the re-check can see that clears what it must hold by half again, and that 1.5 is a rule of thumb, not a prediction. Without one, the card says so before the click: it may not hold, or the re-check may not settle it. A study from before this says what's missing. The card, the panel, the chat reply and the 3D view after a carve all read the same choice, and the card says what it gives up.
