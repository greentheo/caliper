### 2026-11-12 · CALI-169 (#259) · A bore goes all the way through

From #257's review (Rule 6). `largest_through_bore` read holes that aren't through. intake and refine write
`cable_fits: largest_through_bore >= 8`, so a grommet with a 14 mm counterbore over a 6 mm hole passed, a false green.
What it read before:

| shape | read |
|---|---|
| a counterbore, 8 through and 14 × 4 deep | 14 |
| a blind 8 mm hole | 8 |
| a 20 mm pocket over a 6 mm through hole | 20 |
| an inside fillet, r5, on an L bracket | 10 (a concave fillet looks in) |

And a 5 mm-deep blind hole's depth read 8, the face's bounding box.

**A bore is now:**
1. **A full turn.** The inward-looking cylindrical faces of one cylinder (a seam can split it into two) are grouped by
   radius and axis line, and kept only where together they span 2π. That drops a fillet or an arc.
2. **Measured along its axis.** Its depth is its extent along the axis, from the surface's own V range, and its centre
   is the middle of that.
3. **Through, or not** (`Hole.through`): a ring of points at 0.9 of its radius, plus its axis, carried along the axis
   across the whole part and a millimetre past, meets no material in the part's mesh. A counterbore narrows below
   it, a blind hole stops, and a pocket sits over a smaller hole, so none of those passes.

`largest_through_bore` is the largest bore that's through: the counterbore reads 8, the blind hole 0, the pocket over a
6 mm hole 6, the fillet nothing, and the grommet 6, so `>= 8` fails it. `holes()` still lists blind holes, with
`through` False, for `holes_matching` and alignment.

Stacked on #257 (a tube's outer wall isn't a bore).

**Tests:** 5 more in `test_tube_bore.py` (9 with #257's), all five fail on #257's head. All 6 mutants are caught. The 12 files that read holes pass, 223.

**Rework from the review (the foreman's three, and one found on the way).**
1. **Coaxial holes stay separate.** Faces on one axis line are joined into a bore only where they meet along it, as a
   seam's two halves do. Pinned: a U-bracket with two coaxial pairs of 5 mm holes has 4 holes, each 4 mm deep, and
   `holes_matching(5, count=4)` passes, as on main. Grouped by line alone, it had read 2.
2. **A slot reads 0**, since its round ends are half turns. That's the safe side; a cable through a slot is another
   rule. The docstring says so, and it's pinned.
3. **The through test runs once per geometry**, cached on it (`Geometry._bores`) as its mesh is. Pinned: a 100-hole
   plate reads 6 mm, with 100 holes, under 0.2 s per warm call.
4. **Found on the way: a posed part.** In an assembly the mesh is in the world frame and the solid in its own, so a
   bore is now moved by the pose before it's tested. Pinned with a posed blind hole, which read as through in the
   wrong frame (its ring lay outside the mesh).

All four fail on the previous head; 4 mutants, all caught. The 12 files that read holes pass, 227.
