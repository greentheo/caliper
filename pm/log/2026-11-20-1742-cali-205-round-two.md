### 2026-11-20 · CALI-205 (#339) · round two: one worst failing case for every line that names a failure

reviewer-2 on #355 at 5544ed8: the case I pinned was fixed, but the same bug was one step on. As soon as a coarse fail
sat beside a fail that wasn't coarse, the lines disagreed:
- **S2:** a settled plate fail at 0.3 beside a coarse FEA fail at 0.4. The refusal cast doubt on the plate's hand check,
  and the design card put the floor's number beside the front's place and doubt.
- **S3:** an unsupported case beside a coarse fail. The refusal named no place.

The causes:
- `worst_failed` only ever saw the coarse fails.
- The refusal's doubt came from the run (`verdicts.run`'s first fail), not its own case.
- The refusal's minimum counted an unsupported case.

**The fix: one definition, `verdicts.worst_fail`.**
- **What it is:** of `(run, case)` pairs, the lowest safety among cases whose own `verdicts.case` is `fails`.
- **What it leaves out:**
  - an unchecked case;
  - any case whose FEA found no support, whatever governs it, as the run's own number (`run_safety`) does.
- **Its own verdict decides the doubt:** "a coarse run: the fail may be the model's" only where that case is coarse.
- **Every line uses it:**
  - the refusal (`already_fails`);
  - `answers.fail_said`, the coarse line both the design card (`strength.coarse_fail`) and the scorecard say, so they
    say it from one place;
  - `strength.case`, the lowest supported case, which is that worst fail by construction.
- `answers.worst_failed` is gone.
