### 2026-11-20 · CALI-218 (#370) · A planned floor or wall panel with no "made" is a part, not the ground

Found building #17 slice B, and it blocks the camper. A plan's part whose id ended in a ground word (floor, wall, ground, room, …) was dropped as "what it stands on" whenever the planner gave it no `made`. A camper's `box_floor` or `cabin_wall` would vanish.

- **`plan._ground`:** a part is ground only when the plan says it's the fixed surroundings (`role: "fixed"`, the person's), its id's last word a ground word, and no `made`. A part to be made is never dropped by its name.
- **#117 and #123's pins all hold:**
  - a fixed `bathroom_floor`, `floor`, `garage_wall`, `back_yard` or `yard_ground` is dropped and said;
  - `floor_lamp`, `wall_shelf` and `ground_stake` stay;
  - a built `retaining_wall` stays.
- **Pinned unchanged:** a fixed `garage_floor` that says it's "poured concrete" stays a reference part.
