### 2026-11-17 · CALI-199 (#334) · round four: a floor is backed only within a voxel of what it rests on

caliper-reviewer's re-check at 07151ac, and the foreman's one item.

**The code was wrong.** For the default floor, the reach was one voxel past the support's box, and the box's top is the
slab's, 0.6 of a voxel over the ground. So a floor read as backed up to about 1.6 voxels over the ground. Since round
three the gap is measured from the ground, so past one voxel the words contradicted themselves: "the floor is 8 mm
above the ground, under one voxel (6.2 mm)". The plate check stayed skipped.

**Now** `_backed` takes each support's gap first (`_gap`, as in round three). A support counts as backing a point only
where its gap is within the reach. A declared support already met that, so the change only bites the default floor's
slab. 8 mm on BOX spans now, and the plate check reads its own number, as at 10 mm.
