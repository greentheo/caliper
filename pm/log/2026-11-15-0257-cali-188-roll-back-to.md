- **A plain "roll it back" undoes the last change, walking back along `restored_from`** (`chat.undo_target`): the version before the last change, where a restore counts as the version it restored.

  - After v6 alone, it goes to v3.

  - **Go back to this** is `POST …/parts/{pid}/restore {version}`, the same `revert_part`, said in the chat's log.
- **A restore re-measures,** as a roll-back always has. Being a new version, it puts every check, Lighten, Carve and plan of the part out of date, by version, as a revise does.
