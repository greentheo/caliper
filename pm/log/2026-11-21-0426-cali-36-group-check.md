### 2026-11-21 · CALI-36 (#17) · slice E1: a group's own "Will it work?", the box checked on the platform

Theo's "yes check the box on the platform", as designed on #17 (comment 7061065079) and OK'd by the foreman. This is
the engine; E2 (joints between parts) and E3 (the API, the card, Things to fix) follow, each from main.

**What it is:** the load study we have, run over a group's parts as one solid. Only its setup is new:
`group_study.prepare_group`, beside `load_study.prepare`, gives the same keys. `check_load`, the plate check,
buckling, the joints and the verdicts run on it unchanged. A part's own check is untouched: no existing file changes.

**What's checked:**
- **A group that mounts on another** is checked with it, held where that one is held. The box's cases go through the
  platform to what the platform stands on.
- **A group that mounts on nothing** is checked alone.
- **A `fixed` part** (the truck bed, #397) is the surroundings: never in the solid, and a support landing on it stays.

**Loads, supports, pieces, posed** (`store.part_pose`, from each part's own frame):
- **Cases:** the group's own parts' strength loads (edits included), one at a time, named by part: "tray: two bags".
  The force turns with its part.
- **Supports:** each part's own. One that lands on another part of the solid is the union's to carry, so it's dropped:
  the tray's floor is the stool's top.
- **Pieces:** named "the tray's floor", each its own material (#348), over every part.
- **The solid:** each part voxelized on one grid, then together. whittle voxelizes by ray parity, so one concatenated
  mesh would leave a hole where two parts overlap; pinned with the tray sunk 5 mm into the stool.

**What the first build got wrong, and why the joints are in E1.** E1 was to leave every joint to E2. The one-part
group's pin caught it: the stool's own check fails at 0.5× (its joints), and its group read 2.14× (the FEA alone). A
group can't read better than its parts' own checks, so each part's own joints (`joint_check.setup`) are posed into the
group: points, directions and piece keys. Now a one-part group reads exactly its part's own check, 0.5× and 1.75×.
**The code was wrong; the test was right.**

**The box on the platform**, on the synthetic pair: "holds, 16 joints not checked by hand". The FEA reads the
platform's 16 joints, each pull waived, as a part's own check does where the hand rule can't follow the path. Never a
bare ✓. Where two parts meet is E2's.

**Kept on the group:** `group_loads/<run>`, never in a part's runs. `members` holds each part's version; it's stale
when any changes, said: "the stool has changed since it was checked".
