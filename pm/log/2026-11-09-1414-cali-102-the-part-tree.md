### 2026-11-09 · CALI-102 (#94, slice a) · The part tree: parts and pieces, named, coloured, shown or hidden
Theo, on the bar stool: "it looks like the part i made is called i_want ... it should have each part in a tree, and the
subcomponents for that part, can toggle them off and on ... relabel them". Built to the mockup he approved.
- **The tree** sits upper left: each part and the pieces `construction()` declares, a colour by material, and under it
  the materials legend with counts. A reference is grey, says so, and can't be renamed.
- **Names are labels** (`caliper.tree`, `DesignPart.label`, `Design.piece_labels`): the id that scripts, history, runs and
  files key on never changes, so nothing saved breaks. Cards, chip, Studies and the build book read the label. A
  one-part design's part takes the design's name when it's first built. Empty goes back to the id.
- **The eye** hides a part or a piece in this viewer only: kept in the browser per design, never sent. The tree says
  "hidden pieces are still checked". Every check runs on the whole part as ever.
- **Click to select** a part or a piece: the chip reads "Bar stool · seat top" and a message about a piece says which.
  **Double-click to rename** (one listener, timed, so a redraw between the clicks can't lose it).
- **The viewport draws pieces** as the checks build them (`wood_solids`, `printed_solid`), each from `GET
  …/parts/{pid}/piece.stl`; "one body" goes back to the single mesh.
- Not in this slice: reparenting and drag reorder (slice b). Gusset plates and coverings aren't rows: they aren't solids
  of their own. Piece labels reach the tree, the chip and the offers, not the cut patterns or shopping list.
- Seen on the stool: its seat reads blue from above, with a brown rim. That's what the checks build: the outline sits at
  the seat's mid-thickness, so the printed rest keeps about 9.5 mm of PETG above the plywood. The tree didn't cause it.
- Tests: tests/test_part_tree.py (31; the page's through node). Mutants (the double-click window, renaming a reference,
  the eye not kept, the still-checked note, a hidden part's pieces) each fail.
