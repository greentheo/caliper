### 2026-11-21 · CALI-185 (#296, slice B2) · drag a load's box over the part, and turn its arrow

Theo, 2026-11-14: "maybe clicking and dragging the load arrow or the 'fixed plane' … change the loading vector direction".
Stacked on B1 (#376).

- **Handles:** every load drawn on the model has two: its box's middle (in its colour) and its arrow's tail (white).
  - **Taken by their place on screen,** the nearest within 14 px. A handle a few mm across is a pixel or two, under its
    arrow's head (found on the walk).
  - **A capture listener on the view** takes the press before OrbitControls sees it, so dragging a handle never turns
    the view. A Pick on the model in progress wins.
- **Moving:** the box keeps its size, and its middle follows the pointer onto the part's own surface where the pointer is
  over it. Elsewhere (or over another part) it slides on a plane facing you through where it was. "where: … to … mm"
  is said in the view bar as it moves.
- **Turning:** the arrow runs from its tail, where the pointer is, to the box, on a plane facing you. Within 8° of a
  named way (down, toward the front, back, left or right) it snaps to it. "which way: 26.6° from straight down, heading
  90°" is said as the picker says it.
- **The rows stay the source.** A drag writes the row's fields as if typed (`writeScenRow`), and the picture redraws
  from them. Letting go keeps them in the editor: "Moved "standing on the top". Save them to keep it." Nothing is
  saved until Save.
- **All in the part's own frame,** as B1: the pointer's point goes through the inverse pose, and a turned direction
  through the inverse rotation.
