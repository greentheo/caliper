### 2026-11-17 · CALI-189 (#322) · round three: a row's own measurement first; the other joint of a pair isn't a rename

caliper-reviewer's re-check at 28b0707, and the foreman's ruling.

**The code was wrong.** `_moved` looked for a covering row of its own kind (the rename branch) before reading the row's own
measurement. A symmetric stool has two front-rail joint rows. Tick only the left one, and the right one still covers the
case. So the left joint, fixed at 2.4×, read "still open: it's measured as 'the joint between the front rail and the
right side' now".

**Now `_moved` reads `_own` first,** wherever it's found:
- **passing:** fixed, with what still fails the case, said ("the case still fails: the joint between the front rail and
  the right side");
- **failing:** still open, with its number.

**Only where its own isn't measured there:**
- a plate or buckling row of its kind for the case is a rename: "it's measured as '…' now";
- a joint is "not measured as fixed", because the other joint of a pair is the normal state of a pair, not a rename.

**The small one:** the plan line said a number twice ("fillet_r: remove the inside corner fillet strips: fillet_r to 0").
`_said_number` uses the choice's own words where they already name it.

**Tests** (test_fix_these.py, 5 new):
- **the left joint at 2.4×** with the right at 1.62× governing: fixed, with the right one said. At 1.7× it's still open,
  measured;
- **the left joint out of the check** (the reviewer's glue block): not measured as fixed, never "measured as" the right;
- **a case's own `fails:` row:** read by its own number ("'a foot' is still 1.6×"), and none once it passes;
- **a renamed plate** with a plate row of its kind: "it's measured as 'the step' now";
- **the plan line's number,** said once.
