### 2026-11-11 · CALI-157 (#223) · Opus 5.5, through one door

Every request to Claude now goes through `caliper/llm.py`: one `DEFAULT_MODEL` (`claude-opus-5-5`), with `CALIPER_MODEL` read when it's asked for. Effort is set each time: high for writing and revising, low for routing and short descriptions, medium for the rest. Thinking counts inside `max_tokens` on 5.5, so the small caps went up (the classifier from 300 to 2000), and a reply cut off is a failure everywhere. A refusal is said in words before the content is read. `part_scripts.md` is cached, authoring streams to 32000 tokens, and server-side fallbacks are on for the models that have them. The planner's forced tool would be a 400 on 5.5, so it's asked to call it, and a reply without one goes back round.

Tests use a fake client only. No live call. Theo's `.env` still says Sonnet 4.6, which takes the same requests without the fallbacks, until the foreman changes it. The whole suite is CI's.
