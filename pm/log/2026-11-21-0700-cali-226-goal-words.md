# CALI-226 (#384): the scorecard says a goal as a goal

— night shift (UI lane), 2026-11-21

**From the tester's walk:** step-stool-5's design view said "SCORECARD · holds 250 lb, 2 times over (assumed)", unscored, beside
a check of 3.8×. The goal's label read as a result: the 2 is what it needs.

**What's built (page only):**
- **An unscored goal is said as a goal:** "must hold 250 lb, 2 times over". `goalSaid` turns a label that opens on a verb it
  knows (holds, carries, supports, takes, bears, weighs, costs, fits, keeps, stays) into "must …". Any other label is
  said as written ("under $50"). The same goes for a goal that's "new since this card".
- **A scored strength goal leads with the number it does:** "✓ **3.8 ×** — holds 250 lb (needs 2 ×)". Its label loses the
  factor it needs, with answers' `_NEED` pattern copied as `NEED`, and pinned to agree on five labels. So the 2 is said
  once, as what it needs. Other goals' scored rows are as they were ("keeps total weight down — 13.1 lb (cap 10 lb)").

**Judgment calls:**
- **"must hold", not a different badge or column.** That's the issue's own wording, and it needs no new UI.
- **Only strength goals lead with the number.** Their label is the only one that carries a number for the same quantity
  (the factor). A weight goal's "keeps total weight down — 13.1 lb (cap 10 lb)" already reads right.
- **The scored row keeps the label's own words ("holds 250 lb"):** with the number first, it reads as a result.
