### 2026-11-12 · CALI-169 (#259) · The through test only where a rule reads it, largest first

The foreman's follow-up after #261. The through test was cached per geometry, but each build makes a new Geometry and
its context worked the bore out eagerly: about 1.7 s on a 100-hole plate, on every build.

- **Lazy in the context.** `largest_through_bore` is a `_Lazy` value in `build_context`, worked out the first time a
  rule's expression names it and kept. A mesh part still reads as not measured, never 0.
- **`holes()` doesn't test anything.** Each hole's `through` is `None` until a reader asks (`measure.is_through`), then
  kept on it. `holes_matching` counts holes and pays nothing.
- **Largest first.** `largest_through_bore` tries bores from the largest down and stops at the first that's through, so
  the 100-hole plate tests one hole, not a hundred. A bigger blind hole is passed over.

**Tests** (in `test_tube_bore.py`): counting the through tests on the 100-hole plate, none for a build whose rules read
only its volume and its holes' count, one for `largest_through_bore`, and none more on a second read. Also the largest
through bore wins wherever it sits, and a bigger blind hole is skipped. The first two fail on #261's head; all 4
mutants are caught. The 14 files that read holes, the evaluator and promote pass, 299.
