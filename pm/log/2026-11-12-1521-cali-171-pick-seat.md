- **The pick reaches an added part.** `turn` passed a pick to a refine, never to `author_part`. Now an added part gets the
  pick and the part it's on.
- **The picked edge is a requirement, told and measured.**
  - The designer is told where the edge is, in the design's coordinates, which are the new part's. It must touch the
    edge along its whole length, within 0.5 mm (`SEAT_MM`).
  - Each round measures it (`on_the_pick`): the edge is sampled along its length, and the worst point's distance to the
    part's surface is the gap. A miss fails the round, and the next round repairs it, told how far off it is and where
    the edge runs.
  - A pick from an older version of the part isn't used: its ids were that version's.
- **A miss after the last round leads the reply, and the turn isn't a success:** "✗ Built fillet_strip, but it sits
  76.75 mm off the edge you picked on stool: not where you asked." No ✓ over it, and no "Every check on this part
  passes". A strip that sits on the edge says so: "It sits on the edge you picked on stool."
