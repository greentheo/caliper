### 2026-11-20 · CALI-216 (#363) · a running job is plain to see: the part greys, with a spinner, a clock and what it's doing

Theo, after Fix these on step-stool-5: "the indicator is really pretty small and hard to see ... making the main part
screen grey and not working with some circle/timer indicator in the upper right corner ... some text below it to say what
it's currently doing".

**The server (design_api).**
- **`_running` lists every job of the design,** not only load, lighten, release and firstrun, so a carve, a cut plan,
  Shape for drag, Fix these and a chat turn are on the record a page draws from (and finds again after a reload).
- **Each entry says `stoppable`:** `STOPPABLE` lists the kinds whose work stops at its next step (`stop=j.check`). A test
  reads the routes to keep that list true.
- **A chat turn says what it works on** (`meta=_on_part(slug, scope)`), as every part's job does. A delete of that part
  during a turn is refused in words ("… is being revised"), where before it waited behind the lock.

**The page.**
- **One overlay** (`#busy`, top right of the view), drawn from what the page follows (`S.tracked`):
  - a large spinner;
  - the clock, `clock()`, moved each second;
  - what's running on which part (`busyTitle`);
  - its step in words, from the job's own progress lines;
  - a bar where the job knows its count;
  - **Stop it** where it can stop, **Cancel** while it waits, and "stopping at the next step…" once asked;
  - the other jobs listed under it;
  - "… waits till it's done. The chat and your other designs still work."
- **The part on screen first:** `busyJobs` puts the selected part's job first, then the oldest. A first build keeps its
  bubble in the thread.
- **Greying:**
  - the view greys under it (`#busyShade`, which lets clicks through);
  - the part's panel greys and goes `inert` with `aria-busy` when the part on screen is the one being worked on;
  - the design's view never greys.
- **For a screen reader:** a polite live line says what's running and its step, only when that changes, not every second.
- **Every job the page starts is followed** (`trackJob`), so it shows at once: carve, cut plan, Shape for drag and a chat
  turn joined check, Lighten, Fix these and the build package.
- **A reload's clock** is the job's own (`elapsed_s`).
- **The small lines give way:**
  - the panel's progress boxes are hidden while its panel is greyed;
  - the line at the top of the panel is the build package's alone;
  - kinds the panels never knew don't get one (they'd have read "undefined of …").

**Found on the walk, fixed:**
- **The panel's state was `.busy`,** the same class as the overlay box (absolute, top right), so the panel jumped across
  the page. It's `.is-busy`.
- **Stop it was redrawn on every event.** A press and its release landed on two different buttons and the click was lost.
  The box is drawn once per shape (job, stopping, waiting, stoppable, whether it has a count, what else runs), then its
  words and bar change in place (`busyUpdate`).
