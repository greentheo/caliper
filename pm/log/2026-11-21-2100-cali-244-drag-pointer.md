### 2026-11-21 CALI-244: a drag follows the finger that took the handle (#403 review)

— night shift (UI lane), on the foreman's word. Stacked on #403 (the drag's press is #403's `scenPress`, not on main yet);
GitHub retargets it to main when #403 merges.

**Why:** #403 ended a drag on any pointerup or pointercancel, and moved it on any pointermove. A second finger on the
screen (a pinch starting, a palm) could move the box, or end the drag when it lifted.

**What's built:** the drag keeps the `pointerId` that pressed the handle. Another pointer's moves, lift and cancel are
ignored, and the first finger's lift or cancel ends it. An event with no `pointerId` (an older browser, or a test's stand-in)
is taken as before.

**Tests:** `tests/test_drag_touch.py`, 2 more (the second finger lifts, or its touch is cancelled): ignored, and then
the first finger's ends it. Both fail on #403's head. test_scenario_handles passes.

**Mutants (2/2 caught):** any finger moves it; any finger ends it.

**Round 2 (reviewer-3).** A second finger pressing a handle mid-drag started its own drag, replacing `S.scenDrag`. The
first finger's moves then landed on the other row's box, and the first row's drag never ended. `scenPress` now leaves a
press alone while a drag is under way, and a press after it ends is a new drag. Pinned. Mutant (no guard): caught.
