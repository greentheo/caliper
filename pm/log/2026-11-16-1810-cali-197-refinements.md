### 2026-11-16 · CALI-197 (#325) · Theo's refinements: Parameters collapses, sections as a sub-accordion, buttons open what they feed

The foreman's comment on #325 (Theo, 2026-11-16), on top of the first round.

**1. Parameters collapses.**
- It's the top section, outside the four questions: its own header with a mark and a one-line summary, "8 numbers · Ply
  thickness 3/4 in". Open by default, kept as it was left.
- **The mark** (`paramsHead`):
  - ✗ where a typed number won't do;
  - ! where one is typed and not rebuilt, a saved one is outside its range, or the part has changed since its check;
  - · otherwise.
- The plain names, as #171 asked, not script names.

**2. Sections inside each card are a sub-accordion** (`ssecs`): each header its mark (✓ ✗ ! ·), its title and its
one-line answer.
- **What is it?:** Size; Rules (✓ all kept, ✗ "1 of 2 broken"); History ("8 versions, now v12"); Advanced.
- **Will it work?:**
  - Will it hold? (the result, its verdict's own words; ! where its check is of another version);
  - Things to fix (the same `#fixBox`; its mark and count once its list is read: "! 1 measured wrong");
  - Make it lighter / Carve it (out of the load view now; ! where its steps are of another version);
  - Test it, Shape it for drag, whittle, Size the frame.
- **What do I buy?:** Built from.
- **How do I make it?:** Made in your shop (✓ all of it / ✗ not all of it); Patterns; Cut plan (! where it's of another
  version).
- **What opens:** one section at a time within a card: the one kept, else the first that needs attention, else the first.
  A card whose sections need a look says "● inside" on its header, so a problem shows with everything closed.
- **The switch:** `SUB_ONE`, one line. False: each section opens and closes on its own, kept per section, open at first
  where it needs attention.

**3. The buttons open what they feed** (`GO_OPENS`, before the job starts, so its progress is in place):
- Check it again → Will it work?, the result (Things to fix beneath it);
- Lighten / Carve / Shape for drag → Will it work?, its section;
- Make the cut plan → How do I make it?, Cut plan.
- Fix these isn't on main (#322's). It's one line of `GO_OPENS` once it is.
