### 2026-11-11 · CALI-159 (#229) · A hand plate-buckling check for panels in compression

A sheet standing in line with a load is now checked for folding by hand (`buckling.py`), as a flat plate in compression: σ_cr = k π² E / (12 (1 − ν²)) (t/b)² (Timoshenko & Gere §9.2). Each condition is taken on the safe side:

- **Loaded edges:** simply supported, never clamped.
- **Unloaded edges:** simply supported where another piece meets them along their length (probed at their quarter points), else free.
- **k by the unloaded edges:** with both held, k = 4 for a long plate; with one free, 0.425 + (b/a)²; with both free, Euler's wide column.
- **Stiffness:** plywood is taken the weaker way, across its face grain.
- **Load:** each wall takes the case's whole factored load.

A wall it can't describe (not a rectangle, holes in it, loaded at a slant) isn't checked and is named in the note. A printed part's walls stay "not checked".
