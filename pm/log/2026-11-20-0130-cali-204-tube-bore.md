### 2026-11-20 · CALI-204 (#336) · Delete's off switch reads a declared tube as its walls, its bore open

Found by caliper-reviewer's round-three re-check of #332. When Delete's off switch checks what else a number changes,
each other declared piece is a solid to measure against (`pieces._prisms`). A tube was a solid rod there, a cylinder of
its half-width with no bore. So a printed plug in its bore read as part of the tube ("plug_l turns it off but also
changes the tube"): a false red that sent the Delete to the designer for nothing.

**The fix:**
- `consistency.shapes` carries a tube's `outer` and `wall`, where it declares them.
- `pieces._tube` sets the tube as its walls, the bore open through its ends, inset as every prism is.
- **Its turn is read off the build.** Caliper's tubes are square, and a tube declares only its centreline, never its turn
  about it.
  - A round bore wouldn't do: a square plug's corners would still sit in the ring.
  - A bore wide enough for any turn leaves no ring on a thin wall.
  - So `_tube` tries each degree of a quarter turn. It keeps the turn that puts the walls beside all four corners
    inside the solid, the deepest, at a quarter, half and three quarters along. Those probes are half a wall in from
    the face and a wall and a half from the corner, so they're in the steel of a welded tube's rounded corner too.
- **Where no turn fits,** or a wall is too thin to inset, it's the solid rod as before, which never misses a cut into
  the tube.
- A stick with no wall stays a rod.

**Pinned** (`tests/test_tube_prism.py`): a 1 in square steel tube, 16 ga, 300 mm, straight and turned 30°:
- a printed plug filling its bore: `plug_l` is the off switch, and `_also` finds nothing else changed;
- a printed clip whose tab is cut into the tube's top wall: "changes the tube", not the switch;
- the prism is the walls at 0°, 30° and 75°: its volume, steel at the corner in, the bore and its face out;
- a tube whose turn the build doesn't show, and a plain stick, stay rods;
- lumber declares no bore.

**Fail first:** the plug pins and the walls pins (5) failed on main. The clip, the rods and the lumber passed on main:
those were already right, and are pinned so they stay so.

**Mutants** (each anchor once, scratch copy), all 8 caught:
1. the tube branch off;
2. no bore;
3. the turn not read (0° only);
4. the probes on the bore's corner (my own first slip, caught before it ran);
5. the bore not inset;
6. `shapes` dropping the wall;
7. a ring with no turn found;
8. the outer not inset.
