### 2026-11-12 · CALI-164 (#244) · A pass can't rest on a check that couldn't see the part

reviewer-2 found this on the bar stool options study, on scratch copies of `i-want` v8. A copy with no openings read **"holds, 3.31×"** while its run was too thin for the voxels. The 3.31 was the plywood seat's plate check, which needs no voxels and governed because it was lowest. The cone's own number, 6.7×, came from voxels that can't see its 5.8 mm wall. A sibling whose lowest number was an FEA pass on the same kind of wall (2.05×) read "not checked". By Rule 6, an unresolved number can't be used to say it isn't the lowest.

- **`load_study.unresolved` counts what the FEA couldn't see, whatever governs.** A case is unresolved when the FEA governs too coarsely, as before. It's also unresolved when another check governs by being lowest while the FEA, which ran, couldn't see a printed wall (`unseen`). A sheet too thin for the voxels is the plate check's to judge, which needs none, so it's no gap. An older run that doesn't say where its thinnest wall is counts as unknown, the safe side. So any unresolved check makes the case "not checked", unless a resolved check already fails: a ✗ stays a ✗.
- **It says which.** The case's verdict carries the words, and the card leads with them: "An adult sitting: not checked: The seat holds 3.31x; the printed walls are too thin to check at this size (5.8 mm, needs 4 voxels across, and these are 2.6 mm)."
