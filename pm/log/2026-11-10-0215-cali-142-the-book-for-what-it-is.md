### 2026-11-10 · CALI-142 (#182) · The build book for what's actually being built

**What was wrong.** A plywood-and-printed bar stool got a trailer's book: "13.3 lb on the tongue", fibreglass and "the first drive", no step for its printed cone, and a shopping list with no filament.

**The change.**
- A design is on a hitch only where a part declares where it's held, as the page's balance panel already reads it. Otherwise the headlines say "all up", with no tongue.
- The Finish step fits what's there: glass, paint or neither; welds only where there are tubes or plates; "the first drive" only on a hitch.
- A printed piece gets its own step: print it, pin it if it's in sections, and fasten it. Caliper hasn't sized those fasteners, and the step says they aren't on the list.
- The shopping list has the filament, by the same weight and price as *What do I buy?*. The book says why the slicer's grams can differ.
- A single panel on something that isn't a vehicle is called by its own name, and isn't told to be screwed to itself.

Made a real book from a copy of the stool: no vehicle or glass words, and 5238 g of PETG filament, the same as the card. The whole suite is CI's.
