### 2026-11-14 · CALI-183 (#294) · Right-click a part, in the view or the tree, for a menu: delete, rename, hide, ask, check, …

Theo, 2026-11-14: "I should also just be able to right click a part and delete it, or select it in the parts browser and do the same … There should be a menu of options when right clicking on either." On 11-13, removing a stray part took him three tries through the chat (#287).

- **One menu, opened from either place.**
  - **The 3D view:** a right-click that didn't drag (right-drag still pans), or a Ctrl-click. It opens on the button's release, since macOS fires `contextmenu` on the press.
  - **The tree:** its row, by right-click.
  - **A phone:** a long-press (`longPress`, 550 ms held still) in either place; the lift's click doesn't also select.
  - **The keyboard:** Shift+F10 or the menu key opens it for the selected part, the arrow keys move through it and Escape closes it. Delete on a selected part opens the delete confirm straight away.
  - **Closing:** a click elsewhere, or Escape.
- **What's in it.** Each item does what its panel or the chat already does:
  - **Delete…:** the page's own confirm, never the browser's: "Delete inside_corner_fillets? It goes to the design's trash; you can put it back." Then a bar offers **Put back**.
  - **Rename…:** the tree's inline rename (it opens the tree if it's folded).
  - **Hide / Show** (the eye's own `toggleHidden`), **Show only this**, and **Show everything** when anything's hidden.
  - **Ask about this:** `setScope`, the chip, with the cursor in the box.
  - **Check it holds**, **Lighten it** or **Carve it:**
    - offered from `studies.menu`, the same rule as the panels and the Studies page;
    - greyed, with why, when it can't run here;
    - Lighten or Carve as `made_of` says, and neither where it says none (Theo's PLA strips get no Lighten);
    - it runs as the cards' `useBetter` does.
  - **Mirror it…** fills the message box with the chat's route ("Make … a mirror of "). **Break the mirror (of …)** is the tree's `unmirror`.
  - **Versions…:** the part's History (the heading has `id="partHistory"` now).
  - **Copy its name.**
  - **A piece's row** gets Rename…, Show only this and Ask about this.
- **Delete and Put back are two new routes,** since removal went only through the chat:
  - `POST /api/designs/{slug}/parts/{pid}/remove` calls `trash.remove`. It's refused in words while a job works on that part ("inside_corner_fillets is being checked: wait for it, or stop that first."). A mirror's source is refused with "side_right is mirrored by side_left: delete both, or break the mirror first?"; the confirm then offers Delete both, Break the mirror, or Keep it.
  - `POST /api/designs/{slug}/trash/restore` calls `trash.restore`.
  - **The chat log:** both write what was asked and what was done, so the history shows it.
  - **The view:** Delete and Put back take the part out of the view and put it back, as a refresh does.
- **`studies.menu` knows a mirror can't be lightened or carved** ("it mirrors side_right: change side_right and it follows, or break the mirror first"). The server already refused it with a 409.
- **Done-when, in the browser on a scratch copy of step-stool-5 (:8016):**
  - right-click `inside_corner_fillets` in the tree, Delete, Delete: gone, in the trash, and said in the chat; Put back restores it;
  - right-click the strip in the view, then Enter, Enter by keyboard: gone, then back with Put back, and the view follows each time;
  - a touch long-press on a tree row opens the menu without selecting the part.
