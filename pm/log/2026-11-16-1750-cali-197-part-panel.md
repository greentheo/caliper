### 2026-11-16 · CALI-197 (#325) · the part panel: Parameters on top, the four questions as an accordion, the actions top right

Theo's asks after taking step-stool-5 to its cutouts. Layout only: no number, check, study or file changes.

**1. Parameters at the top.** `paramBox` sits under the part's name and version, always in view; the design's own view
puts it at the top too, where it has one part. `cardsView` no longer places it.

**2. The four questions as an accordion.**
- Each header is an `h3` holding a real button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) with its mark. Its answer lines stay in
  view, so a closed card still says how it stands.
- Opening one closes the others; the open one closes again on its own header.
- **On first load:** the first ✗, else Will it work?. The answers arrive after the first draw, so the cards follow the
  first ✗ when they come, unless a card was kept.
- **Kept per design and part** (`caliper.open.<slug>/<part>`).
- **Found on 8016:** the answers' repaint set each card's class afresh, dropping "open" (and "focus", the same before).
  It keeps both now, pinned.
- A link to a panel opens its card and every closed block it sits in. A job's progress in a closed card goes to the top
  line, as one in a closed block did.

**3. Blocks one level down,** closed until opened, each kept open or closed (`caliper.sub.<what>`), each summary saying
what's in it:
- **What is it?:** Size (its size), Rules on this part (how many), History (how many versions, which now), Advanced;
- **Will it work?:** Test it, Shape it for drag (how many searches), whittle carving, beside Things to fix, Scenarios,
  Joints by hand and Other thicknesses, which were already blocks;
- **What do I buy?:** Built from (the stock and what to buy);
- **How do I make it?:** Patterns for hand tools; The cut plan (made or not), with Assembly order inside it.

**4. The actions, small, top right, beside the name:**
- **The buttons, in the chain's order:** Check it again, Lighten or Carve (whichever the part's `study` says), Shape for
  drag (where it moves through air), Make the cut plan. They keep the panels' own ids (`lGo`, `lightGo`, `carveGo`,
  `aGo`, `cpGo`), so their wiring, progress and job states are as before; the in-card buttons are gone.
- **Greyed** from `design.studies.menu`'s `ready`/`why`, the same the right-click menu reads, so the two can't disagree.
  `aria-disabled` with the reason on hover. On a click or a tap, a capturing listener on the bar says why below it, and
  the button's own handler never runs.
- **The chain, in `studies.menu`.**
  - "it doesn't build yet";
  - then, where it has loads, for Lighten, Carve, Shape for drag, whittle, Patterns and the cut plan: "check it holds
    first" (no check of this version), or "it doesn't pass its loads yet: 1.10 where it needs 1.3" (a failing one).
  - Only a fail holds them back, as Lighten itself refuses one. A check that can't settle (Theo's unsized strips)
    doesn't.
  - A study's own reason comes first, where it has one.
  - Called with no check, as before, the menu asks only the build.
- **Not in this PR:** Fix these is #322's and not on main yet. It joins the bar when that merges.

**Phone width:** the buttons wrap under the name, and the accordion is full width.
