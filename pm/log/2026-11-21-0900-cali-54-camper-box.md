### 2026-11-21 · CALI-54 (#1) slice C · A camper box's walls, roof and clear space, written from the chat and checked

Theo's next build is a truck camper: a platform, and a foam-core box on top. The designer could write what a sheet-goods part is built from and what it must hold, and both were checked. It wasn't held to the declarations a foam-walled box needs: the walls as deep beams for the frame study (`shell()`) and the space it keeps clear (`keeps()`). And the skins on a sheet had no agreed form for the load study (#351). The foreman OK'd it on #1.

- **The skin declaration** (agreed on #351 with night shift, so #351 reads what the designer writes). A sheet's `covers` entry is a name, as before, or `{"material", "t", "face"}`.
  - A skin is an `M.SKINS` key whose entry isn't None (`paint` is weight only). `t` defaults to the table's.
  - `"out"` is the face the `outline` is drawn on, `"in"` the face `thickness` away. With no face, the first entry is out and the second in.
  - `M.skins_of(item)` reads it. `M.cover_name` reads an entry's material for the take-off, the build book and the kid's book, so a dict entry doesn't break them (on main it raised "unhashable type: 'dict'"). A skin's `t` scales its weight.
- **`shell_in_walls`, a round check:** each wall beam lies in a wall `construction()` lists (sampled along its foot, a little up the wall), of that wall's core and thickness (0.5 mm), with at least its skins, counted. An extra skin (a corner patch) must be one the construction declares.
- **`keeps_clear`, a round check:** the shape's surface cuts into nothing `keeps()` holds clear. It's measured as the drag study and the build card measure it (`aero.keep_check`): "the outside cuts 100 mm into the mattress".
- **Rounds see them:** the sandbox now reads `shell()` and `keeps()` with the other declarations, so a mismatch fails the designer's round like any failing measurement.
- **The reference** (`part_scripts.md`) gains the dict cover form and a worked foam-core box (construction with skins, shell, roof, keeps). It says both checks.
