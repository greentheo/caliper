### 2026-11-20 · CALI-183 (#294) · Copy… and Move… on a part's menu, placed by a typed form

Theo, 2026-11-14: "maybe a copy and move or specify a location to put the copy on". #302 built the rest of the menu and
left these two to this second PR.

- **A part's place is its pose in `assembly.yaml`** (`position` mm, `rotation` degrees, three.js XYZ). Every check already
  builds it there (`measure.pose_matrix`), so a move needs nothing new in the engine. `placing.py` holds what's new:
  - **`placed(store, pid, how)`:** one of `offset`, `at` (where the middle of its box goes) or `beside {part, side}`
    (touching that part's box on that side, the other two directions as they were), plus a `turn` of 0/90/180/270
    about x, y or z. The turn is about the part's own middle, so it turns in place. Refused in words: two ways at once,
    nothing to do, a number over 100 m, a bad side or axis, a part that isn't there, a part beside itself.
  - **`move`:** writes the pose, keeps `{kind: pose, from, to}` in the part's history, and re-measures a skeleton size
    measured off the part, as a revision does. No new script version: the script didn't change. Put back is a move with
    `to`, the pose it was found at.
  - **A mirror and its source aren't moved alone** (409, with why): the mirror sits where its plane puts it, and
    wouldn't follow.
  - **`copy`:** `free_id` (`step_stool_2`, never an id a part in the trash keeps); `own_script` (a mirror's made its own as
    Break the mirror does, and `FOLLOWS = {}`, so its numbers are its own); its spec, carved mesh, loads as edited,
    grain and piece names come along, copied, not shared. It keeps the original's role and lands beside it in the tree.
    It's named "<label> 2" when the original has a name. With no placement, it goes beside the original on +x. A bad
    placement writes nothing.
  - **`measured`:** the part against every other built part, where it now sits. An overlap gives its volume (over
    1 mm³); a gap of 0.5 mm or less is a touch. A pair that won't build is said as not measured.
- **Routes:** `POST …/parts/{pid}/move` and `…/copy`. They're refused while a job works on the part, hold the design's
  lock, and write the request and the reply to the chat log.
- **The view draws a moved part again,** though its version is the same: each part now carries `pose` and `drawn` (the
  version, plus a hash of the pose once it's moved). The mesh, the pieces and `stl_url` key on `drawn`. A part never moved
  keys as it always did.
- **A pick from before a move** is dropped ("Moved: click it again"), and its faces and edges are asked again. Their
  cache keyed on the version alone, and the server's `features.json` already keys on the pose.
- **The page:** Copy… and Move… on the part menu, before Versions ▸. Move… is greyed, with why, on a mirror and its
  source. The form sits in the menu box (as a dialog), in the design's units. The arrow keys stay in its boxes. It
  says "Copying, then measuring where it lands…" while it works, and a refusal stays in the form. A move offers
  Put back; a copy selects the new part and its words stay until closed.
