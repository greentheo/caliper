### 2026-11-11 · CALI-153 (#217) · A part spanning between held patches can't read a false green

The strength check holds a part's `held()` patches fixed. A 3/4 in plywood shelf on two patches at 350 lb read "holds" 5.06, where beam theory gives about 1 across the grain. Two changes:

1. A panel resting on `held()` patches at two opposite edges now gets the beam check, and all round the plate check, as a panel on other pieces does. The shelf now governs by the beam: about 1.0, doesn't hold. The default floor doesn't count as resting on: anything would rest on that.
2. Where a load lands between separate holds and the FEA is the only check (printed spans, sticks, a panel the beam check can't take), the verdict is a new kind, `span`, "not settled", with the reason. No ✓ and no number on the card, the goals, the scorecard, the build book, Studies and carve and lightening steps. A load past its holds (a bracket's arm) or over one of them (a leg pushed down) doesn't span. A ✗ stays a ✗.

Before and after, on every sample and on copies of Theo's designs: no verdict changes. Each that has a check stands on one patch of floor, so nothing spans. Sample stool 3.1x holds; step-stool copy 3.1x holds; `i-want` 1.6x doesn't hold; `step-stool-2` check it again. How supports are modelled is Theo's call after the coupons. Sticks don't get a beam check here, only the hedge. The whole suite is CI's.

Rework after the review: `span` applies only under need × 3.2 (`verdicts.SPAN_MARGIN`, the worst the check read high in the coupon table, to be revisited when Theo's measured coupons come in), so a solid block at 38 times reads as it should. A span is now found from the load's own footprint: a load spread over three feet spans, and feet joined into one patch span, while a leg pushed straight down and a ring or box's walls don't. Every reader goes through it: the trial panel (no break load, no plan past what it must carry), a breaks goal with the right reason, the strength panel, carve, Lighten, the scorecard row (no number), and its own count in the summaries.
