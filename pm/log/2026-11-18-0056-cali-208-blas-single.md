### 2026-11-18 · CALI-208 (#347) · every entry that solves runs its BLAS on one thread

reviewer-2 found it on greentheo/whittle#9. With this env's OpenMP OpenBLAS, solves running at once in one process come
back wrong while reporting converged: a residual of about 0.6 against an rtol of 1e-5, off by up to 39%, already so at
25e282e. Only CI pinned BLAS to one thread (CALI-114). The served app runs a study per request on a thread, so two
studies at once on 8011 could mis-solve.

**The fix:** `import caliper` sets `OMP_NUM_THREADS`, `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS` and `MKL_NUM_THREADS` to 1 before anything
in it imports numpy.
- `caliper/__init__.py` imports numpy through `assembly`, so the CLI's own module is already too late; the package is
  where it goes.
- Every entry starts by importing the package: the served app (`caliper.cli serve`, `caliper.server`), the CLI, and the
  build and sandbox workers.
- It's set, not defaulted: a shell asking for 8 threads gets 1.
- Where numpy was loaded before Caliper, threadpoolctl (installed, not a dependency) holds its pools to one thread too.

**Pinned** (`tests/test_blas_threads.py`): each entry imported fresh in its own process, with nothing asked and with 8
asked. All three variables read 1, and every pool threadpoolctl finds is at one thread. That includes a process that
loaded numpy and scipy first.

**Fail first:** all 10 failed at main. **Mutants:**
1. the variables defaulted rather than set: caught (6 of 12 cases fail, where 8 is asked);
2. the threadpoolctl hold dropped: caught (the numpy-first cases).

**Measured here** (this Mac, the caliper env, whittle 25e282e), LEGGED and BOX_STOOL strength checks:

| BLAS threads | LEGGED alone | BOX_STOOL alone | both at once on two threads | same as alone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 6.1 s | 4.1 s | 8.6 s | yes |
| 1 | 5.2 s | 3.2 s | 8.3 s | yes |

- **Speed:** one thread was 1.2–1.3× faster on these small parts. reviewer-2 measured 2.5–17× on their whittle tables.
- **Not reproduced:** two whole studies at once came back identical to each alone, at 12 threads too. The mis-solves
  are reviewer-2's measurement, on bare solves hammered from many threads. The pin goes in on theirs; I didn't see them
  on these two parts.
