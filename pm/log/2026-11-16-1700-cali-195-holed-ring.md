### 2026-11-16 · CALI-195 (#323) · the check of a cut step reads as the step did

Theo's test: Lighten's step 14 on step-stool-5 v7 read 1.47 standing; the check of the shape it cut (v8) read 1.10 and
failed. I reproduced both on copies (Theo's shop file copied to scratch, whittle 25e282e, min safety 1.3): Lighten's step
14 at 1.47, FEA 3.16; the check of v8 at 1.10, FEA 3.43. Both match his runs.

**What differed, at step 14 against the check, standing on the top:**

| | Lighten | check of v8 |
|---|---|---|
| k (plate check ÷ the panel's FEA, solid) | 1.612 | 1.612 |
| edge (the solid panel's stress at the holes) | 3.164 | 3.162 |
| the ring's voxels | 3440 | 3440 |
| the panel's voxels left solid | 24382 | 24262 |
| **its peak, outside the ring** | **5.61** | **8.40** |

So k, the edge and the ring's size agree, and the peak doesn't.
- **Why the holes differ.** Lighten cuts its circles' cells itself. The check voxelizes the rebuilt mesh, and v8's holes
  fall on the grid differently: they lose 200 voxels just outside the circles (19.2 to 20.3 mm from their centres; the
  circles are 19.05) and keep 80 inside. That looks like the panel's columns sitting about half a voxel off whittle's
  grid.
- **Why the peak differs.** The check's ring, the voxels left out of the peak round each hole, came from the circles'
  cells. The stair-stepped voxels at the solve's real hole edge counted in the peak.

**The fix.** In `judge_holed`, the ring is round the holes as the circles' cells have them **and** as the solve has them
(`_gone_ring`: the panel's voxels filled in but not in the solid). Lighten cuts the cells themselves, so this is the ring
its step left out.
