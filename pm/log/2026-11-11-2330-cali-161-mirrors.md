### 2026-11-11 · CALI-161 (#239) · Mirrored parts: one script, flipped

On step-stool-4 the sides weren't mirrors: walls 15 against 12 mm, another pocket depth, a boss against a pad. The designer also spent a full try writing a part that's a flip.

- **A mirror's script is generated, never designed.** It's the source's script, whole, with a wrapper (`mirror.py`) that does two things:
  - flips what it builds, with CadQuery's own `mirror`, in a plane (`YZ`, `XZ` or `XY`) through `MIRROR_AT`;
  - flips the points and directions it declares, in its construction's outlines and facets, sheet holes, stick ends, `at`, `HOLES` (with their normals) and load boxes, whose `lo` and `hi` are re-sorted.

  Sizes (`thickness`, `size`) are never flipped. So every reader of a part's script (the cut list, holes, joints, the strength study) reads the mirror as a part of its own, with no change to them. Measured on a side with a pocket and a boss, its volume agrees with its source's to 0.1%.
- **It follows its source.** `DesignStore.write_part` writes a part's mirrors again whenever it's written, and a restore goes the same way. A mirror of a mirror is refused.
- **An edit asked of the mirror goes to its source:** "side_left mirrors side_right: I changed side_right, and side_left follows it (v3)."
- **One part for both, in words:** "let's choose the right side (the one without the hole) to use for both sides" (Theo's), "use the right side for both", "make side_left a mirror of side_right", "mirror the right side onto the left side".
  - The other side becomes the mirror in one round, with no designer call.
  - Its plane is half way between the two parts, on the axis they're furthest apart on.
  - Its own script is kept in its versions.
  - While a plan is under review, the plan edit handles it instead.
- **Breaking it:** "make the left one different", "stop mirroring the left side" or "unmirror side_left" keeps the flipped shape as a script of its own, which no longer follows.
- **In the plan:** a plan part can be `mirror_of` another, with `mirror_plane` and `mirror_at` (the origin unless given). The planner is told to set it for a pair, matching sides, "the other side" or "mirror". It's built after its source, by flipping, not designed. A mirror of an unknown part, of itself or of a mirror is refused.
- **What it says when it's made:**
  - Its pattern pages say "a mirror of side_right: flip its template".
  - A printed mirror's making plan says its STLs are the source's, mirrored, or that the source's files can be flipped in the slicer.
  - The card's print line says so too.

**Rework from reviewer-2's review (the foreman's five).**
1. **Nothing done to a mirror is lost silently.** `DesignStore.write_part` refuses any write of a mirror's script except by the mirror itself: "that would be undone the next time side_right changes: side_left mirrors side_right: change side_right and side_left follows it, or break the mirror first". That covers a restore to an old mirror version too.
   - A Parameters edit on a mirror is made on its source, and says so: "side_left mirrors side_right: I changed side_right, and side_left follows it". Pinned: change side_left, then side_right, and nothing is undone.
   - A carve, a Lighten or a picture on a mirror is refused up front, in the chat and the API, with "break the mirror first" and how.
2. **No mirror of a mirror, however it's reached.**
   - Turning a source into a mirror while something mirrors it is refused: "side_right has a mirror, side_left: break that first, or make side_left a mirror of side_extra too".
   - A cycle names both parts: "side_right and side_left are already one script: side_left mirrors side_right". The plan says "'a' and 'b' each mirror the other: one of them has to be the source".
   - Asking again writes nothing: "already mirrors side_right: nothing to change".
3. **Only what's named, and asking above a pair.** "For both" takes the source's like-for-like twin only, the same extent on each axis give or take 20%. "For all", "every" or "each", or more than one twin, asks first: "Make panel_bottom a mirror of panel_top? It'd lose its own script (kept in the versions)". A yes does it. A request about a size ("pick the top panel thickness for all panels") isn't a mirror request.
4. **The promote guard checks claims, not words.** A name claims a mirror only when it names two parts (two ids, a plural of two siblings, or two position words) and uses a claim word (mirror, match, same, identical, flipped, twin, pair, alike) about their shape, not a size or a load. `symmetric_load_ok`, `width_same_as_base`, `mirror_frame_wall` and `side_left_same_width_as_side_right` pass; `sides_match` with no `mirrors()` is refused.
5. **Smaller:**
   - Piece and hole names flip with the part: left and right across YZ, front and back across XZ, top and bottom across XY.
   - The parts tree marks a mirror ("a mirror of side_right: change it there") with a "break the mirror" button, backed by a new endpoint.
   - `mirrors()` measures in mm: the largest distance between the two surfaces, sampled both ways, passing under 0.2 mm, in place.
   - `mirror_off` takes the OCCT lock.
