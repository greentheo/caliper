### 2026-11-21 CALI-235 (#17's page part): the plan's groups as nodes in the part tree

— night shift (UI lane). This is the design on #17, with the foreman's OK. Membership stays the plan's, because slice E's
group check reads it.

**Why:** #369 (slice A) gave a plan groups, sub-assemblies inside one design (Theo's camper, "a platform plus a box on
top"), with `Design.groups` as the tree's contract.

**What's built:**
- **Server:** the design's state carries `groups` (`[]` without).
- **The tree:**
  - **A group is a node** above its members, two deep at most. It has a caret to fold it (kept per design with the
    tree), an eye to hide or show every part in it as one (off only when every one is hidden), and its name (its id in
    words, the plan's `what` under it) with its count ("Box · 3 parts"). It's a `treeitem` with `aria-level` and
    `aria-expanded`.
  - **Inside a group:** its inner groups, then its own parts in the person's order (#94). A part sits under another only
    inside the same group (`treeOver`). Parts in no group come after the groups, as now.
  - **Moving:** a drag, Alt+arrows or Move in the tree ▸ works within a part's group. Alt+← never takes it out of its
    group. A move across groups is refused in words at the page's foot: "The platform's parts are the plan's: to move
    one into or out of it, say it in the chat." A drag across one draws no drop line, but it's taken so it can be said.
- **Without groups:** a design draws byte for byte as before. That's pinned on main's own treeHtml output, recorded for a
  design with nesting, a hidden part, pieces and a reference, with and without a `groups: []`.

**Judgment calls:**
- **Inner groups before a group's own parts.** The plan holds them in build order, but the person's order is for parts.
- **The refusal is said at the page's foot** (announced, as a key move's words are), not in the tree's note as the design
  said.

**Walked** on 8021 with a scratch camper: Platform (deck, joists), Box (box_floor, and Walls: left_wall, right_wall), and
the ladder in no group.
- The tree drew them so; Platform's eye hid both its parts and showed them again; folding Box hid Walls and its parts.
- Alt+↓ on the platform's last part said "joists is already at the bottom"; Alt+↑ moved it and said "Moved joists up,
  above deck".
- A drag of deck onto box_floor was refused in words, and the order was unchanged.

**Tests:** `tests/test_group_nodes.py`, 15 (13 fail on main; the 2 byte-for-byte pins pass there by design). The tree
harnesses in test_tree_keys, test_tree_moves, test_part_tree, test_part_menu and test_mirrors name the four new helpers.

**Mutants (11/11 caught):**
- nesting across groups;
- the plan's order, not the person's;
- no inner groups;
- the fold ignored;
- the eye off when any part is hidden;
- the group's eye shallow;
- a move across groups made;
- the keys crossing groups;
- a silent refusal;
- no state field;
- ungrouped parts first.
