### 2026-11-20 · CALI-36 (#17) slice A · A plan's parts in groups: sub-assemblies inside one design

Theo's next build is a truck camper: "a platform plus a box on top, built in one go". Slice A of #17's design (the
foreman's OK; Theo: "two deep is fine").

- **`Plan.groups`:** `[PlanGroup{id, what, parts, mounts_on}]`.
  - A group holds parts or groups, two deep at most (`DEEPEST`).
  - It mounts on a group or a part ("box" mounts on "platform").
  - A part may mount on a group too.
- **Refused in words** (`_check_groups`, and `order`):
  - a group holding nothing, itself, or a name the plan hasn't got;
  - groups holding each other in a circle;
  - three deep;
  - a part in two groups;
  - a group mounting on what it holds, or on a name the plan hasn't got;
  - a part mounting on the group it's in;
  - a name used twice across parts, groups, skeleton numbers and measures;
  - a mount cycle through groups, said by its parts and groups ("box_floor → platform → deck → box → box_floor").
- **The build order:** everything a group mounts on is built before any of its parts, and its own parts go in the plan's order. The camper builds legs, rails, deck, cleats, then the box's five parts.
- **The floor dropped from a plan** (#117) leaves its group too.
- **Saved in the plan:** `design.plan["groups"]`, only where there are any. `Design.groups` reads it there, `[{id, what, parts, mounts_on}]`, or [] without. That's the contract for the page's tree (caliper-ui) and for slices B and E. design.yaml gains no key, so a design without groups is byte-for-byte as it was.
- **The review says them:** "A slide-in camper in 9 parts, in 2 groups: the box, the camper box (…); the platform, the bed platform (…)". A plan without groups says what it said before.
- **The planner** is told about groups: only where the design is more than one thing of several parts each, two deep at most, with numbers still in the one skeleton. Its tool's schema takes them.

**Tests:** 17 in `test_plan_groups.py`, on a synthetic camper plan (no live call). All 17 failed on main. All 13 hand-made mutants are caught:
1. a part ignores its group's mounts;
2. no group checks;
3. three deep allowed;
4. a part in two groups;
5. the floor stays in its group;
6. groups not saved;
7. groups not loaded;
8. a part on its own group;
9. a group mounting on an unknown name;
10. the headline without groups;
11. a group holding an unknown name;
12. a circle of groups not refused;
13. a flat plan gaining a `groups` key;

`test_plan`, `test_plan_numbers` and `test_plan_groups` pass, 93.
