### 2026-11-12 · CALI-18 (#23) · A tube's outer wall isn't a bore

Found while authoring a grommet: `largest_through_bore` read a tube's outside (40 mm) as its bore, because both are
cylinders. `holes()` already said it reports "each interior cylindrical face"; it reported every one.

A cylindrical face is a hole only where its outward normal points at its axis, so the material is outside it
(`measure._looks_in`, from the face's own orientation on the B-rep). A tube's outer wall, a rod and a boss look out, so
they're left out. That holds for every reader of `holes()`: the bore, `holes_matching`, and hole alignment between parts.

**Tests:** 4 in `test_tube_bore.py`: a tube (20 mm, not 40), a plate with a real 8 mm hole, a solid rod (no bore) and a
grommet (a flanged tube, 12 mm). Three fail on main. Both mutants (the filter dropped, the sign flipped) are caught.
The 12 files that read holes pass, 218.
