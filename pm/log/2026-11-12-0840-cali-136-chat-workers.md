### 2026-11-12 · CALI-136 (#174) · Chats that wait hold no worker

Before hosting. Studies and first builds already ran on workers of their own (#205). That left two ways for chats to
fill the 8 workers another chat needs, one person's or another's:
- **A chat that turns into a study** (a carve, a picture) took the one-study-at-a-time turn inside the chat job, and
  waited for it while holding a chat worker. Eight of those, and a ninth chat waited.
- **A chat queued behind another job on its design** waited on that design's lock, also while holding a worker.

**Now waiting takes no worker.**
- Chat jobs run on threads (up to `WAITING_THREADS` = 256, cheap while they wait). Each takes one of the 8 workers (a
  slot) only once it's past its design's lock, to do the work.
- A job that has to wait for the study turn gives its slot back (`Job.park`, found through `jobs.current()`). It runs
  its study under the one turn, which is the limit that matters for a study.
- Working chats are still held to the worker count.

**Tests:** 4 more in `test_waiting_studies.py`.
- With 8 or 12 chats waiting for the study turn, another chat starts within 2 s.
- With 10 chats queued behind one design's job, another design's chat starts.
- Nine working chats never run more than 3 at a time on a 3-worker runner.

The first three fail on main; 330 pass across the 14 files that use a runner or the study turn. All 5 mutants are caught: the slot never taken, the park dropped, the slot taken
before the design's lock, the slot never given back, and the job not marked current.

The error-scrub sweep, #174's last comment, comes as its own PR next.
