**2. The off switch, measured exactly.**
- **How it's measured** (`_also`): manifold3d booleans of the build now and the build at 0. What it adds is
  `difference([at0, now])`. What it takes is `difference([now, at0])`, intersected with each other declared piece's solid
  (`_prisms`: a sheet's outline extruded through its thickness on the side the solid is on, and a stick as a cylinder;
  each inset 0.5 mm, never more than a quarter of its thickness, so a neighbour's face against it isn't in it).
- **Which piece it names:** added material inside another piece's solid "changes the {piece}"; added outside every one
  "adds to the part where nothing is declared"; taken from inside one "changes the {piece}".
- **The bar is `CHANGE_MM3 = 1.0` mm³, from the noise as measured** (scratch noise.py):

  | build at 0 | added | taken from other pieces | compare |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | STRIPPED fillet_r (a true switch) | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 1 ms |
  | TWO_JOB strip_w (a true switch) | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 4 ms |
  | NOSE_W nose_w (a true switch) | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 4 ms |
  | step-stool-5 v010 fillet_r, its curved strips (a true switch) | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 6 ms |
  | NOSED nose_r at 3 mm (the reviewer's case) | **147.39** | 0.0000 | 5 ms |
  | THINS fillet_r (the top 6 mm thinner) | 0.0000 | **217,805** | 1 ms |

  The booleans add no noise on any of these, so 1 mm³ sits above zero and far under the smallest real change (147 mm³,
  a 3 mm rounding of a 3/4 in sheet's corners). It doesn't grow with the part, as the grid's cell did.
- **Where the builds run:** the compare is milliseconds, but the builds still aren't (1.2–1.6 s each here, 3.5 s at the
  reviewer's nice 15). So they run on the Delete click only.

    or fillet_r at 0 may turn them off. Each is built first to be sure (a few seconds, no Claude); where none only does
    that, it's the designer's instead." It names no single number (fillet_gap only looks right). Where the click has
    been built, it says what it found.
  - **POST delete builds, then sets the number**, or returns the designer's plan and why with nothing set and nothing
    logged. The page then asks again with that why, and the second Delete sends the designer the piece's key.
  - **Things to fix (`fixes._removed`) reads only what's known** (`pieces.known`, never a build). Until a Delete… has been
    built for this version, it offers the designer's way. This is a judgment call: Theo's strips show the designer
