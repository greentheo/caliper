### 2026-11-15 · CALI-185 (#296) · Slice A: a part's scenarios and supports as data in design.yaml, edited as text in the strength panel

Theo, 2026-11-14: "I can click on 'show the stress' on every one of the 3 scenarios (and I probably should be able to edit the scenarios or add new ones)". He then widened it: the "doesn't move" fix point and the loading should be "correctable, either with user input as text in the right hand menu …". On a standard's case: "lowered, default to the standard, allow it to be edited".

This is stacked on #311 (CALI-190, the joint check), because joints read the same supports and directions. It merges after #311.

- **Data** (`scenarios.py`, `Design.scenarios`):
  - **Where they live:** a part's scenarios and supports, as the person edits them, are kept in `design.yaml`.
  - **Who reads them:** `load_study.prepare` reads them in place of the script's `loads()` and `HELD`. So every strength check, Lighten and carve follows, and the joint check, which reads the same loads and supports.
  - **With no edit,** the script's very items are read: byte for byte as before (pinned).
  - **What isn't edited here:** tipping cases stay the script's.
- **A scenario:** what, how much (lb, kg or N), which way (a unit vector, down by default), margin (×), where (the box it pushes on), and on or off.
- **A standard's case** keeps its numbers as the script gave them.
  - **Edited,** it's named for what it is now: "EN 14183, edited: 100 kg (the standard is 150 kg)". It never says "its rating" or that it meets the standard.
  - **"Back to the standard"** puts it back, name and all.
  - **Turned off,** it's said on the card in amber: "Not checked: its rating (EN 14183, 150 kg) is turned off.". One of the person's own, turned off, is said as information.
- **A support:** a box, or the floor at its underside, as a part holds by default.
- **An edit writes a new version of the part,** the same script (action `loads`, with the count in its history line).
  - **Why a version:** 31 places in the Python and 13 on the page judge a run by its version against the part's. A version made by the edit makes every check, Lighten and carve of the part read "older version" through the path they already have. Lighten's and the carve's adopt refuse them too. Nothing keeps a verdict from loads that are gone (Rule 6).
  - **"Back to its script's"** is a version too.
- **Refused in words, nothing written,** where a row can't be acted on: no name; how much or its margin not more than nothing; which way, nothing; a unit that isn't lb, kg or N; where it pushes, or where it's held, not touching the part (by `load_study.touches`); every scenario turned off.
- **The run records it:** `loads_edited`, and `loads_off` by name.
- **Routes:**
  - `GET /api/designs/{slug}/parts/{pid}/scenarios`: each row with `said`, the name its checks use;
  - `PUT` the same, with `{scenarios, supports}`;
  - `DELETE` the same: back to the script's.
- **The page:** "Scenarios and supports" in the strength panel, under least safety, loaded when opened.
  - **Each row:** what, how much, which way (down; toward the front, back, left or right; or an angle from straight down and a heading), margin, where (two corners, mm), and on.
  - **A standard's row** shows its checked name read-only, what its standard is, and "Back to the standard".
  - **What holds it:** its rows, the floor, and Add.
  - **Buttons:** "Save them", and "Back to its script's".
  - **A field left as it was keeps its exact value:** corners are shown to 0.001 mm. Saving had rounded 19.05 to 19.1, so a standard's case put back read as edited. The browser check found that.
- **In the browser** (:8016, a scratch copy of step-stool-5 v7):
  - lowering the rating to 100 kg saved as v8; "Check it again" read the case as "EN 14183, edited: 100 kg (the standard is 150 kg)", 220.5 lb × 1, with nothing claiming the standard;
  - "Back to the standard" and Save gave v10, named "its rating (EN 14183, 150 kg)" again;
  - the parts list followed each version;
  - at 375 px nothing scrolls sideways.
