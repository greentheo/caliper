### 2026-11-10 · CALI-88 (#64) · A picture's edge in a wall is judged as a hole's

#64 item 5, the third of #64's PRs. A T-rex cut out of a leg panel used to get the voxel FEA alone, which reads low at a sharp re-entrant edge, while a top's hole-saw holes got K_t = 2 at their edges. Taking rule 6's safe side, as the foreman decided, a picture's wall is now judged like a holed top. Its edge is checked at 2 times the stress the wall had there when solid. The finished part's check (`lighten.judge_holed`) does this, and so does the picture's own search while it shrinks the picture (`pictures._try`), so the two agree. The rest of the wall is the FEA's, as before, and the plate check of the panel stood on still applies, since that panel has no holes.

Before and after, on the sample stool (200 lb adult stepping up, needs 2):

|---|---|---|---|
| T-rex on the left side | 3.11 (plate) | 3.11 (plate) | 24.1 |
| T-rex on the front | 3.11 (plate) | 3.11 (plate) | 6.73 |

No verdict moves on the sample: the top's plate check governs both. The edge is closest on the front, where the panel carries the step's load down to the floor. A picture whose edge reaches up into where the wall works harder reads lower, and a test shows it. The whole suite is CI's.
